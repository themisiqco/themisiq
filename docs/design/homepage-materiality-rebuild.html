The four decisions, resolved

  • Two tiers became one. Paper against the band measures 1.29:1 and sunken 1.12:1 — neither reads as a distinct surface, so there was no way to keep two tiers with light fills. Instead the six credibility cards stop being cards. Scenarios stay cards because they are three parallel, comparable things with figures; the credibility points are a ruled definition list, which is what six unlike statements actually are.
  • The violet left border is gone with the cards. It was the card's only accent, and once the card becomes a list row a hairline rule does the same job without needing a colour at all.
  • The scenario ramp is blue → rust → crimson — #1C5EAA, #A94E0D, #A41A3B, all already in the palette as the CBAM, Climate and Cyber module hues. It reads cool to warm, which is the sequence the content needs, and measures 6.50 / 5.55 / 7.53 on white. The colour is on the top rule and the figure, not the fill, so the three cards stay legible and the ramp is visible at a glance. Note this only works on white — rust drops to 4.29:1 on the teal band, so this section must not sit on one.
  • The two bands are now one component. Same markup, same treatment, brand left edge. They were previously the same shape in two colours, one dark and one white, which is why converting either alone would have made them disagree.
  • The section sits on the page, not in a panel. A 2px ink rule opens it. Full-bleed dark sections and full-bleed tinted sections both say "marketing band"; a rule and a heading say "next section".
